# Proof Demonstration

Use with the shared reference, decomposition, pattern, fact mapping, contract, and experiment skills, plus `factframe-script-storyboard` and the product-fact claim audit.

## Procedure

1. Extract the proof device and list input, operation, observed phenomenon, display surface, and allowed conclusion separately.
2. Design the smallest target proof protocol that demonstrates the mechanism without importing the reference product's unsupported claims.
3. Bind every conclusion to a target Product Fact or mark it as a non-claim observation.
4. Keep units, numbers, comparisons, safety, efficacy, and medical language under explicit Fact and Promotion gates.
5. Freeze proof mechanism as the only Creative Dimension, then run Media QA and independent experiment reconciliation.

## Stop conditions

- a physical analogy is being treated as efficacy proof;
- a measurement lacks source, unit, method, or Product Fact binding;
- the proof requires a claim not present in the target Snapshot;
- the run has only one sample but the request asks for causal or universal proof.

## Completion criterion

Every proof step has a recorded observed phenomenon and allowed conclusion, unsupported proof claims are zero, and the final finding preserves the method limitation and sample scope.
