# Experience POV

Use with the shared reference, decomposition, pattern, fact mapping, contract, and experiment skills, plus `factframe-script-storyboard` and the AI Media QA SOP.

## Procedure

1. Extract POV, sensory, action, scene, prop, sound, subtitle, and continuity cues with time ranges.
2. Separate experience mechanism from product efficacy, health, safety, parenting, or comparative claims.
3. Rebuild the target POV using authorized or controlled assets. Replace protected surface identity while preserving the declared sensory function.
4. Bind any product claim to target confirmed/locked Facts; leave emotional or sensory observations as experience language unless a fact supports more.
5. Freeze experience as the only Creative Dimension and run ASR/OCR, product identity, continuity, rights, and experiment gates.

## Stop conditions

- experience language is being used to imply unsupported product effect;
- product identity changes across frames or components;
- audio, text, asset rights, or critical inspection is unavailable without human fallback;
- POV or ASMR is not actually the primary mechanism.

## Completion criterion

The target preserves the declared POV/sensory function with a different authorized surface, continuity and identity checks are independently recorded, and no unsupported product claim survives.
