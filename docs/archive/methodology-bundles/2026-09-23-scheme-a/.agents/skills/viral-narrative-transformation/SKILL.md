# Narrative Transformation

Use with `viral-reference-intake`, `viral-content-decomposition`, `viral-pattern-abstraction`, `viral-fact-mapping`, `factframe-script-storyboard`, `viral-replication-contract`, and `viral-experiment-evaluation`.

## Procedure

1. Require a DecompositionMap showing timed Problem/Tension → Intervention → Result → CTA beats.
2. Preserve the narrative relationship, not the reference wording, performer, product identity, or unsupported benefit.
3. Translate the target product into a new scenario using only current confirmed/locked Facts and server-owned creative fields.
4. Compile duration and shot budgets through the existing Creative Pattern contract. Do not use free-form duration or copy as a safety mechanism.
5. Freeze the narrative change as the only Creative Dimension and route to the shared Replication Contract.

## Stop conditions

- the narrative requires an unsupported efficacy, comparison, safety, price, or promotion claim;
- the reference is actually proof, experience, conversion, or trend-led and cannot be separated;
- target scenes introduce persona or affordability assumptions not supported by the contract.

## Completion criterion

The adapted script has the same declared narrative function with materially different surface expression, every product claim is fact-bound, and the contract contains one narrative Creative Dimension only.
