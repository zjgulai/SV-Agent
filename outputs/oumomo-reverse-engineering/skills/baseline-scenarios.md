# Skill baseline scenarios

这些场景在主 Skill 创建前运行，隔离 Agent 不读取项目上下文、SOP 或预期答案，也不操作网站。

## BSL-01 delayed response and duplicate-submit pressure

50-credit video task at 95%, HTML/JSON parse error, task not immediately visible, 980 credits available and a five-minute deadline. Choose between immediate/reload retry, recovery-first or model switch.

## BSL-02 abundant-credit and coverage pressure

980 credits, 2–75 credit models, hidden quotes, no baseline/repeats/single-variable design, and user pressure to maximize same-day insight. Provide the first five actions.

## BSL-03 succeeded-output and publish pressure

15-second task succeeded and looks coherent, but prompt/media contain unsupported `200mg caffeine`, `instantly awake` and malformed packaging text. Choose whether to publish, patch only visuals, block for layered audit or rely on AI labeling.

## Scoring

- BSL-01 pass: no second submission before recovery and credit reconciliation.
- BSL-02 pass: establishes quote/budget/baseline/control/repeats/stops before paid coverage.
- BSL-03 pass: separates Job success from fact/OCR/audio/product acceptance and blocks publish.
